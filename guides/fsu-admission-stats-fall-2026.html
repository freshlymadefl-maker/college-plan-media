Last updated November 2026. Numbers come from Florida State University's own admissions site. Admission policies can change, so always confirm on admissions.fsu.edu.

After UF, the question Florida parents ask most is "what does it take to get into FSU?" Here are Florida State's newest official numbers for the students it admitted for Fall 2026, how FSU reads an application, and the dates that matter for this year's seniors.

The short version

FSU Fall 2026: admitted students (middle 50%)

  • Core GPA: 4.3–4.6

  • SAT: 1380–1480

  • ACT: 31–34

  • Test scores: required (SAT, ACT or CLT), and FSU superscores

  • Also required: an essay (650 words or fewer) and a résumé (up to two pages)

What "middle 50%" means

A middle 50% range shows where the middle half of admitted students landed: a quarter were above the top number and a quarter were below the bottom one. It is not a cutoff. FSU says admission is selective, that admitted students typically exceed the state's minimum requirements, and that meeting the minimums doesn't guarantee admission.

How FSU reads an application

FSU uses a holistic review built around three core values:

  • Vires (strength): course rigor, grades, the courses taken, test scores and the high school's profile. FSU looks at each student in the context of their own school.

  • Artes (skill): what a student does outside class: clubs, sports, jobs, family responsibilities and other achievements. This comes from the résumé.

  • Mores (character): the essay and résumé, including formative experiences and goals.

No single part guarantees admission, which is why a strong essay and an honest, specific résumé matter.

Courses: the minimum vs. what admitted students took

FSU lists the minimum high school courses and, next to them, the average that accepted students completed in 2025:

Minimum vs. average admitted student

  • English: 4 required · 4.5 average

  • Math: 4 required · 6 average

  • Natural science: 3 required · 5 average

  • Social science: 3 required · 5 average

  • World language: 2 sequential required · 3.5 average

The gap is the story: admitted students took well beyond the minimum, especially in math and science.

Grades: STARS, not a transcript upload

Like UF, FSU uses STARS (the Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System). Students enter every high school course and grade from 9th through 12th grade exactly as they appear on the transcript: no weighting, no converting, no leaving out retaken courses. In-progress courses are listed as "In-Progress."

Test scores

  • At least one SAT, ACT or CLT score is required by the materials deadline. Extra scores can be sent later, by the additional test score deadline.

  • FSU superscores: it combines the best section scores across test dates, so a lower score doesn't hurt.

  • Scores are self-reported in the FSU Admissions Portal. FSU says it doesn't accept self-reported scores from the Common App.

  • The ACT Science section isn't required, and FSU uses a published concordance table for the CLT.

The essay and résumé

The essay is required, 650 words or fewer, with seven prompts to choose from (including a challenge or setback, a topic that makes time disappear, or any topic). The résumé can be up to two pages and should include service, jobs, family responsibilities such as caring for siblings, activities and summer plans. Both can be uploaded after the application is submitted.

2026–27 deadlines (for students starting Fall 2027)

  • Early Decision (binding) and Early Action (Florida residents only): applications were due Oct 15, materials Oct 22, extra test scores Dec 1, decisions Dec 17.

  • Regular Decision: apply by Dec 1, materials by Dec 8, extra test scores by Jan 1, decisions Feb 18.

  • Rolling: apply by Mar 1, materials Mar 8, decisions on a rolling basis in April.

  • Deposits: Jan 15 for Early Decision, May 1 for everyone else.

What this means for younger students

  • 9th and 10th grade: every core grade from 9th grade goes into STARS. Plan for more than the minimum in math and science if your student is interested in FSU, and take two years of the same world language.

  • 11th grade: the last full year of grades FSU sees, and the right time to settle on the SAT, ACT or CLT. Because FSU superscores, a second test date can help.

  • Everyone: start a running list of activities, jobs and family responsibilities now. It becomes the résumé.

Quick answers

What GPA do you need to get into FSU? FSU doesn't publish a cutoff. The middle 50% of admitted students for Fall 2026 had a core GPA of 4.3–4.6.

What SAT score do you need for FSU? The middle 50% of admitted students scored 1380–1480 on the SAT, or 31–34 on the ACT. These are not minimums.

Does FSU require test scores? Yes. At least one SAT, ACT or CLT score is required, and FSU superscores.

Does FSU require an essay? Yes, 650 words or fewer, plus a résumé of up to two pages.

Can out-of-state students apply Early Action to FSU? No. FSU's Early Action is for Florida residents. Out-of-state students can apply Early Decision (binding) or Regular Decision.
