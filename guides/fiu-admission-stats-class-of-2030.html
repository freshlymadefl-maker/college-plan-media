FIU, in Miami, is one of Florida's biggest public universities and a top choice for many South Florida families. Here are FIU's newest official numbers, what FIU asks for in an application, and the dates that matter for this year's seniors.

The short version

FIU fall admits: middle 50%

  • Weighted GPA: 4.1–4.7

  • SAT: 1250–1380

  • ACT: 27–31

  • CLT: 84–95

  • Test score: required (SAT, ACT or CLT)

  • How to apply: the Common App, $30 fee (or an SAT/ACT fee waiver)

Source: FIU Admissions FAQ ("Fall Admits" middle 50%; FIU doesn't list the year on that page).

The Class of 2030, by the numbers

FIU News reported in August 2026 that more than 5,400 first-time students started at FIU in summer and fall 2026. Students who started in the fall had an average GPA of 4.32 and an average SAT of 1296, and 88% had a GPA above 3.75. The class came from 883 high schools and 62 countries. Those are averages for enrolled students, so they're a little different from the admitted middle-50% ranges above. Source: FIU News, "Class of 2030: Raising the bar on excellence" (Aug 2026).

What "middle 50%" means

The middle 50% is where the middle half of admitted students landed. A quarter of admitted students were above the top number and a quarter were below the bottom one. It's a guide, not a cutoff.

Fall vs. summer: two different profiles

Like several Florida public universities, FIU admits some freshmen to start in the summer. The summer middle 50% is noticeably lower: a 3.9–4.4 weighted GPA, 1100–1200 SAT, 22–24 ACT and 70–83 CLT. FIU's FAQ lists an overall acceptance rate of 50%: 33% for fall and 68% for summer. If your student is offered a summer start, that's an admission, and it's worth taking seriously. Source: FIU Admissions FAQ.

Test scores

  • FIU says an SAT, ACT or CLT score is required for first-time-in-college admission. Students can apply before scores arrive, but FIU won't make a decision until it has them.

  • FIU recommends testing in junior year and no later than the fall of senior year.

  • ACT: for the 2025–26 application, FIU's ACT superscore uses English, math and reading (science dropped).

  • Scholarships: FIU takes the best individual sections of the SAT or ACT for scholarship review.

Source: FIU Admissions FAQ and First-Year Applicants page.

Transcripts

FIU asks for official transcripts from every high school a student attended. Its first-year pages don't describe a self-reported grade system like UF's or USF's STARS, so plan on sending official records. Source: FIU First-Year Applicants page.

How to apply

FIU applications go through the Common App, which opens August 1. FIU says not to submit a separate application through its own portal. The fee is $30, or students can use an SAT or ACT fee waiver. Early Action at FIU is non-binding. Source: FIU First-Year Applicants page and FAQ.

Fall 2027 deadlines

FIU first-year dates (complete application with documents)

  • Early Action: Nov 2, 2026 · decisions Dec 10, 2026

  • All first-year applicants: Dec 16, 2026 · decisions Jan 21, 2027

  • Final deadline: March 2, 2027 · last decisions April 2, 2027

  • Enrollment deposit: May 1, 2027 ($200 or a deposit waiver; nonrefundable, applied to tuition)

Source: FIU First-Year Applicants page, "Important Dates & Deadlines." Every date is also in our free Florida college deadline calendar.

FIU Honors College

FIU's FAQ says students are considered for the Honors College with a weighted GPA of at least 3.8 plus an SAT of 1290, ACT of 27 or CLT of 90. Honors applicants also submit a personal statement and résumé through MyFIU. Source: FIU Admissions FAQ.

What this means by grade

  • Seniors: the Dec 16 deadline is the next one. Make sure test scores are sent, because FIU won't decide without them.

  • Juniors: pick the SAT, ACT or CLT and test this spring. The summer and fall ranges are far apart, so a higher score can change the start term.

  • 9th and 10th grade: core grades and course rigor build the weighted GPA, and two years of the same world language are required at Florida's public universities.

Quick answers

What GPA do you need to get into FIU? FIU doesn't publish a cutoff. Its fall admits' middle 50% weighted GPA is 4.1–4.7, and fall 2026 starters averaged a 4.32.

What SAT score do you need for FIU? The fall middle 50% is 1250–1380 on the SAT, 27–31 on the ACT and 84–95 on the CLT.

Does FIU require test scores? Yes. An SAT, ACT or CLT score is required, and FIU won't make a decision until it arrives.

Does FIU use the Common App? Yes. FIU applications go through the Common App.

When does FIU release decisions? Dec 10, 2026 for Early Action, Jan 21, 2027 for the Dec 16 deadline, and by April 2, 2027 for the final deadline.

What's FIU's acceptance rate? FIU's FAQ lists 50% overall, 33% for fall and 68% for summer.
