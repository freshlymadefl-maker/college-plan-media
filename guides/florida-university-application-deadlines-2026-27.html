Last updated October 2026. Deadlines can change, so always confirm on each university's official admissions page before your student applies.

Florida has 12 public universities, and almost every one sets its dates a little differently. Some have Early Decision, some have Early Action, some use a "priority" deadline, and many have a second "materials" deadline a week or two after the application is due. That second date is the one families miss.

Here's every Fall 2027 first-year deadline we could confirm on each university's own website, school by school, for seniors in the Class of 2027.

The short version

The dates most Florida seniors need

  • Oct 15: UF Early Decision; FSU Early Decision and Early Action; UCF and FAU Early Action

  • Nov 1–2: UF, FGCU, New College and FAMU Early Action (Nov 1); FIU and USF Early Action (Nov 2)

  • Nov 15–18: Florida Poly Early Action I (Nov 15); UNF priority deadline (Nov 18)

  • Dec 1 – Jan 15: FSU Regular Decision (Dec 1); USF Regular Decision, FIU and UNF standard (Dec 16); UF Regular Decision (Jan 15)

Early Action vs. Early Decision vs. "priority"

  • Early Action means applying early and hearing back early, with no commitment to attend.

  • Early Decision is binding. Of the 12 public universities, UF and FSU offer it. If admitted, your student commits to that school.

  • Priority deadlines (like UNF's) work much like Early Action: apply by the date to hear back sooner.

School by school

University of Florida (UF)

  • Early Decision: apply Oct 15, materials Oct 22, decisions Dec 11

  • Early Action: apply Nov 1, materials Nov 8, decisions Jan 22

  • Regular Decision: apply Jan 15, materials Jan 22, decisions Mar 19

  • Apply through the Common App. UF's self-reported grades record, STARS, is due by the materials deadline for your option.

Florida State University (FSU)

  • Early Decision (binding): apply Oct 15, materials Oct 22, decisions Dec 17, deposit Jan 15

  • Early Action (Florida residents only): apply Oct 15, materials Oct 22, decisions Dec 17

  • Test scores for Early Decision and Early Action can arrive by Dec 1

  • Regular Decision: apply Dec 1, materials Dec 8, decisions Feb 18; rolling deadline Mar 1

  • FSU accepts the Common App or the FSU Application; Early Decision applicants must use the Common App.

University of Central Florida (UCF)

  • Early Action: apply Oct 15, materials Nov 15, decisions Dec 4

  • Regular: application and materials by May 1, decisions on a rolling basis

University of South Florida (USF)

  • Early Action: apply Nov 2, materials Nov 16, decisions Dec 9

  • Regular Decision: apply Dec 16, materials Jan 15, decisions Feb 10

  • Scholarship consideration deadline: Jan 15. Rolling deadline: Mar 1.

Florida International University (FIU)

  • Early Action: complete application with documents by Nov 2, decisions Dec 10

  • All first-year applicants: Dec 16, decisions Jan 21

  • Final deadline: Mar 2. FIU uses the Common App.

Florida Atlantic University (FAU)

  • Early Action: apply Oct 15, materials Oct 22, last test scores considered Nov 20, decisions Dec 4

  • Rolling: through Mar 12

University of North Florida (UNF)

  • Priority: application and materials by Nov 18, decisions Dec 9 at 5pm

  • Standard: Dec 16, decisions Jan 13

  • Final: application Apr 1, materials Apr 15

University of West Florida (UWF)

  • At our October check, UWF had not yet posted its Fall 2027 deadline page. Its school counselor page lists Dec 1 as the scholarship priority application deadline, with decisions on a rolling basis.

  • Students use UWF's own application and link their self-reported academic record (SSAR). Confirm dates on uwf.edu.

Florida Gulf Coast University (FGCU)

  • Early Action: apply Nov 1, supplemental items Nov 15, decisions in December

  • Regular Decision: Mar 1

New College of Florida

  • Early Action: apply Nov 1, documents Nov 15, decisions by Dec 15

  • Regular Decision: apply Apr 1, documents Apr 8

  • Common App or the New College Application, no application fee. A transcript or STARS record is required.

Florida A&M University (FAMU)

  • Early Action: apply Nov 1, materials Nov 14, decisions Dec 4

  • Regular Decision: apply Feb 1, materials Feb 14, decisions Mar 5

  • Rolling: through May 1. FAMU calls STARS its most preferred way to submit grades.

Florida Polytechnic University (Florida Poly)

  • Early Action I: Nov 15. Early Action II: Jan 15

  • Regular Decision: Mar 1. Rolling: May 1, which is also the deposit deadline

Don't miss the second deadline

At UF, FSU, UCF, USF, FAU, FGCU, New College and FAMU, the application and the "materials" (self-reported grades, transcript, test scores or supplemental items) have different due dates. After your senior hits submit, have them log in to each applicant portal and look for a missing-items list.

Quick answers

Which Florida public universities have Early Decision? UF and FSU. Early Decision is binding, so read each school's rules first.

Is Early Action binding? No. Early Action lets your student apply early and hear back early without committing.

What's STARS? UF's Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System, where students enter their courses and grades. UF requires it by the materials deadline, and New College and FAMU also accept it.

When do UF Early Decision decisions come out? Dec 11. UF Early Action decisions come out Jan 22.

Can a student apply Early Action to several Florida schools? Early Action at Florida's public universities is non-binding, so many students apply early to more than one. Early Decision is different: a student can only commit to one Early Decision school. Always read each school's rules.
