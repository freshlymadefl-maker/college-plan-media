UCF is one of the biggest universities in the country and one of the most popular choices for Florida seniors. Here are UCF's newest official numbers for the freshmen it admitted for Fall 2026, what goes into an application, and the dates that matter for this year's seniors.

The short version

UCF Fall 2026: admitted freshmen (mid-range)

  • High school GPA: 4.11–4.57

  • SAT: 1310–1420

  • ACT: 28–32

  • CLT: 91–101

  • First generation: 24% of admitted freshmen

  • Grades: self-reported on UCF's SPARK form

  • Essay: strongly encouraged, not required

UCF lists these as of August 2026.

What "mid-range" means

UCF calls these "mid-range" numbers. A range like this shows where the middle of the admitted class landed: some admitted students were above the top number and some were below the bottom one. It is not a cutoff, and UCF says meeting the minimum requirements doesn't guarantee admission.

The GPA UCF uses

The GPA on your student's report card is probably not the one UCF uses. UCF recalculates using academic core courses, and grades in AP, AICE, dual enrollment, honors and IB core courses "will be given additional weight." That's why so many admitted students have GPAs above 4.0. If your student is in 9th or 10th grade, the takeaway is simple: grades in core classes count from 9th grade on, and challenging core classes are weighted.

Minimum courses (18 units)

UCF's minimum high school units

  • English: 4 years

  • Math: 4 years (Algebra 1 and above)

  • Science: 3 years

  • Social studies: 3 years

  • World language: 2 years of the same language, in sequence

  • Academic electives: 2

These are minimums. Most admitted students go beyond them, especially in math, science and advanced courses.

SPARK: UCF's self-reported grades

UCF doesn't start with a transcript upload. Students fill out the SPARK form (Self-Provided Academic Record for Knights) with their high school courses and grades. UF, FSU and USF use a similar system called STARS, so if your senior is applying to several Florida schools, expect to enter grades more than once. Students who have already graduated from high school also send a current official transcript.

Test scores

  • UCF asks for official SAT, ACT or CLT scores.

  • SAT and ACT scores are valid if taken within the last five years. CLT scores must be from July 1, 2023 or later.

  • ACT superscore: for ACT tests taken April 2025 or later, UCF builds the composite from English, Math and Reading (Science is optional). If all of a student's ACT tests were taken before April 2025, UCF uses all four sections.

The essay, fee and how to apply

The essay is strongly encouraged but not required. If your student has a story that the grades and scores don't tell, it is worth writing. The application fee is $30 (nonrefundable), and students can apply with either the UCF Undergraduate Application or the Common App.

2026–27 deadlines (for students starting Fall 2027)

  • Early Action: the application was due Oct 15. Materials (SPARK + test scores) are due Nov 15. Decisions come Dec 4: admit, Pathway Admit, defer or deny.

  • Regular Decision (fall): application and materials by May 1 (summer: Mar 1). Rolling decisions start in January.

  • Scholarships: Early Action applicants who meet every deadline get priority for merit scholarships, and UCF says that money is usually gone by mid-January. Applying early matters.

What this means for younger students

  • 9th and 10th grade: core grades count from the start, and weighted core classes help. Plan two years of the same world language.

  • 11th grade: the last full year of grades UCF sees before senior fall, and the time to settle on the SAT, ACT or CLT.

  • Everyone: keep a running list of activities, jobs and awards. Even with an optional essay, a clear picture of your student helps.

Quick answers

What GPA do you need to get into UCF? UCF doesn't publish a cutoff. Admitted freshmen for Fall 2026 had a mid-range high school GPA of 4.11–4.57, using UCF's weighted calculation.

What SAT score do you need for UCF? The mid-range for Fall 2026 admitted freshmen was 1310–1420 on the SAT, 28–32 on the ACT and 91–101 on the CLT. These are not minimums.

Does UCF require an essay? No. It is strongly encouraged but not required.

What is SPARK? The Self-Provided Academic Record for Knights, UCF's form for self-reporting high school courses and grades.

When does UCF release Early Action decisions? December 4 for students who met the Oct 15 application and Nov 15 materials deadlines.
