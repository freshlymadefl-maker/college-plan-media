Last updated November 2026. Numbers come from the University of South Florida's own admissions site. Admission policies can change, so always confirm on usf.edu/admissions.

USF, with its main campus in Tampa, is one of the most popular choices for Florida seniors. Here are USF's newest official numbers for the freshmen it admitted for Fall 2026, what USF actually asks for in an application, and the dates that matter for this year's seniors.

The short version

USF Fall 2026: admitted freshmen (mid-range)

  • High school GPA: 4.13–4.58

  • SAT: 1290–1420

  • ACT: 28–32

  • CLT: 92–100

  • Grades: self-reported in STARS

  • Essay: none. USF doesn't request an essay or personal statement

  • Application fee: $30 (fee waivers can be requested)

Source: USF Office of Admissions, Freshman Academic Requirements page, admitted freshman profile.

What "mid-range" means

A mid-range shows where the middle of the admitted class landed. Some admitted students were above the top number and some were below the bottom one. It is a guide, not a cutoff. USF also says fall is its most competitive term.

Fall vs. summer: two different profiles

USF publishes a separate profile for students admitted to start in the summer. For Summer 2026, the mid-range was a 3.71–4.29 GPA, 1140–1230 SAT, 23–26 ACT and 72–84 CLT. If your student's numbers are closer to the summer range, that is worth knowing when you read a decision letter or plan a backup. Source: USF Freshman Academic Requirements.

The GPA USF uses

The GPA on your student's report card is probably not the one USF looks at. USF recalculates a GPA from grades in core academic subjects (plus certain AP and IB fine and performing arts courses), and adds extra quality points for advanced courses when the grade is a C or better:

USF's added weight for advanced courses

  • AP, IB, AICE and dual enrollment: +1.0

  • Honors: +0.5

That's why so many admitted students have GPAs well above 4.0. Source: USF Freshman Academic Requirements.

Minimum courses (18 units)

USF requires at least 18 approved units in the core subjects: 4 English, 4 math (including one above Algebra 1), 3 natural science, 3 social science, 2 world language and 2 academic electives. USF says its most competitive applicants usually complete at least 20 academic units. The two years of world language need to be in the same language. Source: USF Freshman Academic Requirements.

STARS: USF's self-reported grades

Students at U.S. high schools don't upload a transcript at first. Instead, they complete STARS (the Self-Reported Transcript and Academic Record System) through the USF Applicant Portal, listing every high school course and grade. Senior-year courses go in as "In Progress." A few tips from USF:

  • Enter grades exactly as they appear on the transcript or report card. USF warns that inaccurate entries can cost a student their admission or a scholarship.

  • The application isn't complete until STARS and test scores arrive.

  • If admitted, the student sends a final official transcript after graduation, and USF checks it against STARS.

Source: USF STARS page.

Test scores

  • USF requires official scores from at least one of the SAT, ACT or CLT, sent directly from the testing service.

  • Superscoring: USF "considers your highest submitted section scores across all SAT, ACT or CLT test dates."

  • Minimum section scores: SAT 490 Reading and Writing and 480 Math; ACT 19 Reading, 17 English and 19 Math; CLT 38 Verbal plus Grammar/Writing and 16 Quantitative Reasoning.

  • The SAT and ACT essay sections aren't considered.

  • Timing: USF tells summer and fall applicants to test before December so scores arrive in time for a decision.

Sources: USF Freshman Academic Requirements and How to Apply pages.

How to apply

Students can use USF's own application or the Common App. USF says it has no preference between them. The fee is $30 (nonrefundable), and students can request a fee waiver in the Applicant Portal; waivers are reviewed case by case. USF doesn't request an essay or personal statement. Source: USF How to Apply.

2026–27 deadlines (for students starting Summer or Fall 2027)

USF freshman dates

  • Early Action: application Nov 2, 2026; materials (STARS + test scores) Nov 16, 2026; decisions Dec 9, 2026.

  • Regular Decision: application Dec 16, 2026; materials Jan 15, 2027; decisions Feb 10, 2027.

  • Scholarship consideration: Jan 15, 2027.

What this means for younger students

  • 9th and 10th grade: core grades count from the start, and honors, AP, IB, AICE and dual enrollment courses earn extra weight at USF. Keep two years of the same world language on the plan.

  • 11th grade: the last full year of grades USF sees before senior fall, and the time to take the SAT, ACT or CLT. Because USF superscores, more than one test date can help.

  • Everyone: USF doesn't ask for an essay, so grades, course choices and scores carry most of the weight.

Quick answers

What GPA do you need to get into USF? USF doesn't publish a cutoff. Admitted freshmen for Fall 2026 had a mid-range GPA of 4.13–4.58, using USF's recalculated, weighted GPA.

What SAT score do you need for USF? The Fall 2026 mid-range was 1290–1420 on the SAT, 28–32 on the ACT and 92–100 on the CLT. USF also lists minimum section scores (for the SAT, 490 Reading and Writing and 480 Math).

Does USF require an essay? No. USF says it does not request an essay or personal statement.

Does USF superscore? Yes. USF considers the highest section scores across all SAT, ACT or CLT test dates you submit.

What is STARS? The Self-Reported Transcript and Academic Record System, where applicants list their high school courses and grades instead of sending a transcript first.

When does USF release Early Action decisions? December 9, 2026, for students who applied by Nov 2 and sent materials by Nov 16.
