"What does it take to get into UF?" is one of the most common questions Florida parents ask, and a lot of the answers floating around are years old or come from third-party sites. Here are the University of Florida's newest official numbers for the students it admitted to the Class of 2030 (students who started in 2026), what they mean, and how UF reads an application.

The short version

UF Class of 2030: admitted students

  • Applications: 95,986

  • Admitted: 19,012 (about 1 in 5)

  • Middle 50% GPA: 4.5–4.7 (UF's recalculated core weighted GPA)

  • Middle 50% SAT: 1420–1520

  • Middle 50% ACT: 32–35

  • Admitted through Early Action: 14,497. Through Regular Decision: 4,515.

What "middle 50%" means

A middle 50% range shows where the middle half of admitted students landed. A quarter of admitted students were above the top number and a quarter were below the bottom number. UF says plainly that these ranges are not minimums. A student below the range can still be admitted, and a student inside it isn't guaranteed a spot.

The numbers, compared with last year

UF also published a profile for the Class of 2029. Side by side:

  • Applications: 91,896 (Class of 2029) → 95,986 (Class of 2030)

  • Admitted: 18,169 → 19,012

  • Middle 50% SAT: 1380–1510 → 1420–1520

  • Middle 50% ACT: 31–34 → 32–35

  • Middle 50% GPA: 4.5–4.7 both years

The share of applicants admitted stayed close to 1 in 5, while test scores at the middle of the range moved up. UF also says the Class of 2030 admits came from 3,988 high schools, 58 Florida counties, all 50 states and 74 countries.

Honors students

UF's admitted honors students had a middle 50% GPA of 4.6–4.8, SAT of 1520–1560 and ACT of 35–36.

Why UF's GPA looks so high: how UF recalculates it

UF doesn't use the GPA printed on your student's transcript. It recalculates a core, weighted GPA on its own scale:

  • AP, IB, AICE and academic dual enrollment courses get an extra 1.0, so an A counts as 5.0.

  • Honors, pre-AP, pre-IB and pre-AICE courses get an extra 0.5, so an A counts as 4.5.

That's different from Bright Futures, which adds 0.5 for all of these weighted courses. So the same student can have one GPA for Bright Futures and a higher one for UF. It's also why a 4.5–4.7 range is possible: it reflects a lot of advanced coursework, not just straight A's.

What else UF looks at

UF describes its review as comprehensive, and says no single factor decides an application. Along with the recalculated GPA, UF considers:

  • Rigor: the strength of your student's courses compared with what their school offered, and how they progressed over time

  • Test scores: SAT, ACT or CLT

  • Activities: consistent involvement that shows interests and contributions to school, community or family (family responsibilities count)

  • The essay: UF says it reads for "the quality of thought"

  • Personal background and experiences

The UF timeline for this year's seniors

  • Early Decision (binding): applied by Oct 15, materials and STARS by Oct 22, decisions Dec 11

  • Early Action: apply by Nov 1, materials and STARS by Nov 8, decisions Jan 22

  • Regular Decision: apply by Jan 15, materials and STARS by Jan 22, decisions Mar 19

UF takes applications through the Common App, and every applicant also completes STARS, UF's self-reported grades record, in the Gator Portal. Every date is in our free Florida college deadline calendar.

What this means for younger students

  • 9th and 10th grade: grades in core classes count from the start, and taking the most challenging courses that fit your student matters more than piling on everything. Remember that Florida's public universities expect two credits of the same world language.

  • 11th grade: junior year is the last full year of grades UF sees before an early application, and the right time to settle on the SAT, ACT or CLT.

  • Everyone: pick activities your student actually cares about. UF looks for consistent involvement, not a long list.

Quick answers

What GPA do you need to get into UF? UF doesn't publish a minimum. The middle 50% of admitted students for the Class of 2030 had a recalculated core weighted GPA of 4.5–4.7.

What SAT score do you need for UF? The middle 50% of admitted students scored 1420–1520 on the SAT, or 32–35 on the ACT. These are not minimums.

What is UF's acceptance rate? UF reported 95,986 applications and 19,012 admitted students for the Class of 2030, which works out to about 20%.

Does UF accept the CLT? Yes. UF lists the SAT, ACT or CLT. It doesn't publish a middle 50% range for the CLT.

Does UF count AP classes extra? Yes. In UF's recalculated GPA, AP, IB, AICE and academic dual enrollment courses get an extra 1.0 point, and honors courses get an extra 0.5.
