# Collab outreach (Instagram Collab posts)

A Collab post is one post that is published on both accounts and appears to both sets of followers, with likes, comments and shares combined. It's the fastest free way to put College Plan in front of Florida parents who don't know it yet. In Metricool, add the partner under instagramData.collaborators (username) when scheduling. The partner then accepts the invite in the Instagram app before the post goes live.

## Who to pitch (in this order)
1. **Florida parent and local-mom accounts** (largest parent audiences; they need useful content and we have it). Start in the Tampa Bay area, where the Hillsborough county series starts. Leads to look up on Instagram:
   - Tampa Bay Parenting magazine (tampabayparenting.com), which also publishes a list of Tampa Bay parent influencers
   - Moms of Tampa
   - The Tampa mom-blog lists on Feedspot and 83 Degrees Media name more local parent creators
2. **Local test prep and tutoring businesses** (they want parents of 10th–12th graders; we bring free Bright Futures and SAT-date content). Tampa leads: Florida Test Prep, Tampa Bay Tutors, The Tutoring Center (Tampa), Preble College Prep (Safety Harbor).
3. **Independent college counselors** who sell 1:1 help. We're free information, they're paid guidance, so we complement each other. Find Florida members in the IECA directory (iecaonline.com, search by Florida).
4. **School and community accounts** that post for parents: PTA/PTSA pages, high school counseling-office accounts, local libraries running college nights. These usually prefer a shared post or a resource to share rather than a Collab.

How to vet each lead (5 minutes on Instagram):
- The account posts at least weekly and mostly reaches parents. Ideally it has 1K–50K followers, since smaller accounts say yes more often.
- Real comments, not just emoji.
- Nothing off-brand: no athletics or recruiting focus, no "guaranteed admission" claims, nothing political.
- Note the exact @handle in the tracker below. Handles must be checked in the app; never guess one.

## What to offer (make it easy to say yes)
- **"Florida deadline cheat sheet" Collab carousel**: we design it (their name on the cover slide: "with @partner"), they approve it and it posts on both accounts. Best fit for mom accounts.
- **"Ask a tutor" or "Ask a counselor" carousel or Reel**: they give 3–5 tips, we design it in our template, and both accounts post it as a Collab. Best fit for tutors and counselors. Every fact still gets checked against official sources before it's published.
- **Free tool share**: they post our Bright Futures checker or deadline calendar to their Stories. Best fit for schools and PTAs.
- Offer to send their followers to them in return (tag them, or feature their business on the slide that names a local resource).

Your followers are exactly the parents we make this for. Would you be open to a Collab post? We'd design a free "[Florida deadlines this fall / Bright Futures cheat sheet]" carousel with your name on it, you approve it, and it posts on both accounts. No cost, no strings. Happy to send a draft first! 💙 The College Plan Team

## Weekly routine
- The owner sends 3–5 pitches a week (5–10 minutes). Aim for 1 Collab post every 1–2 weeks.
- When someone says yes, tell Claude their @handle and the format they picked. The weekly content task builds the post and schedules it with them as the collaborator in a normal feed slot (7:00am).
