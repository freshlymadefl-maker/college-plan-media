Bright Futures update

Florida just raised the bar for the Class of 2028.

If your student is a junior this year, the test scores needed for Bright Futures just went up. Here's what changed, and what to do now.

Junior parents: do this now

3 moves to make this fall

Ask for a Bright Futures evaluation

The school counselor can show which award your student is on track for today.

Pick a first SAT date

Dec 5 (register by Nov 20) or March 6 (register by Feb 19). Plan on a possible retake.

Check the hour count

Turn in service hours every year so you're not chasing signatures senior year.
