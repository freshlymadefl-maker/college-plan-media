Good to know

3 things parents ask us

Seniors have until Aug 31

Bright Futures counts tests taken on or before Aug 31 of the graduation year.

Gold Seal Vocational is different

It takes ACT, SAT or PERT scores, not the CLT.

Each university still sets its own bar

Check each school's admissions site for its middle-50% scores.

Source: Bright Futures Student Handbook, Ch. 1
