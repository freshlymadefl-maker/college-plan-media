Sources: FL Board of Governors Reg. 6.002; Bright Futures Handbook

Did you know?

Florida dual enrollment is tuition-free.

Students don't pay registration, tuition or lab fees, and the books are free too. College credit classes need a 3.0 unweighted GPA.

Source: Florida Statutes s. 1007.271
