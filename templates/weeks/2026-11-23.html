Dual enrollment at Polk State

Real college credit, on a high school schedule

$0tuition for dual enrollment students

Polk State serves students from every Polk County public school, most area private schools and home education programs. Florida law also makes textbooks free for public school students.

Sources: polk.edu (Dual Enrollment & Early Admission); s. 1007.271, Florida Statutes

Florida Student Assistance Grant

Florida's need-based grant, FSAG for short

$200–$2,610a year, according to UF's financial aid office

A grant, so it's not paid back. Schools award it from their share of state funds.

Sources: sfa.ufl.edu; s. 1009.50, Florida Statutes

Who qualifies

The checklist

  • Florida residentUF: for 12 months before the school year starts
  • U.S. citizen or eligible noncitizen
  • Financial needUSF: Pell Grant eligible
  • Degree-seeking, 6+ credit hoursat a Florida public university or state college
Sources: s. 1009.50, F.S.; sfa.ufl.edu; usf.edu; onestop.fiu.edu

04 / 08
How to apply

There's no separate form. It's the FAFSA.

Every school we checked (UF, USF, FIU) says the FAFSA is required. USF says to file it by its published priority deadline.

Why early matters: each school gets a set amount of state money for FSAG, so priority dates count.
Sources: usf.edu; sfa.ufl.edu; s. 1009.50, F.S.
