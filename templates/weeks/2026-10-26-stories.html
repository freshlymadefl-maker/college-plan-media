Did you know? · 9th + 10th

The 10th grade PSAT is practice.

The PSAT 8/9 and PSAT 10 are there to see what the test is like. National Merit uses the PSAT/NMSQT that most students take in the fall of 11th grade.

Source: College Board, satsuite.collegeboard.org

Did you know? · Seniors

The FAFSA does not apply for Bright Futures.

That's the FFAA, Florida's own form. The new one opened Oct 1, and it's due by Aug 31 after graduation. Miss it and Bright Futures is off the table.

Source: floridastudentfinancialaidsg.org
