Class of 2027

The Jack Kent Cooke College Scholarship

$55,000per year, for up to four years of college

It's "last-dollar" money: it's added after a college's own aid, to help cover what's left.

Source: Jack Kent Cooke Foundation, jkcf.org (2027 cycle)

Who can apply

Check these 4 boxes

  • Graduating high school in spring 2027and starting at a four-year college in fall 2027
  • 3.75+ unweighted GPAcumulative, not the weighted GPA
  • Family income up to $95,000the Foundation also reviews income and assets
  • At least one SAT, ACT, AP or IB scoreno minimum score required

Plus: all four years of high school in the U.S. Full rules on jkcf.org.

How to apply

It's on the Common App

Two teacher recommendations

One must be an 11th grade teacher in a core subject.

One counselor recommendation

Plus the School Report your counselor submits.

Test score + self-reported grades

Unofficial transcript info goes in the application.
