Advanced Placement

  • College-level classes at your high schoolfrom the College Board, exams every May

Path 4 · Dual enrollment

A real college class, for free

  • Taught by your local collegeon campus, online or at the high school
  • No tuition, free bookspublic school students, s. 1007.271
  • The grade goes on a college transcriptso it starts the college GPA too
Eligibility (GPA + test scores) is set by each district's partner college.
