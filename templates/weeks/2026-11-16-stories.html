Did you know? · 9th + 10th

A part-time job can count for Bright Futures.

Academic + Medallion: 100 paid work hours, or a 100-hour mix of paid and volunteer hours, instead of volunteer hours only. Your district approves how hours are logged.

Source: Bright Futures Student Handbook, ch. 1 (2026–27)
