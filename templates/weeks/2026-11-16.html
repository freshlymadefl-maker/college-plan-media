06 / 08
Florida bonus

The same list helps with Bright Futures.

Bright Futures service hours are earned during high school, and each district approves the activities and how they're logged. Paid work can count too: 100 paid hours (or a mix with volunteer hours) for the Academic or Medallion award.

Ask your district how it logs hours. Source: Bright Futures Student Handbook, ch. 1 (2026–27)

05 / 08
How to apply

Each school runs its own application.

The school's financial aid office sets the steps and the deadline. Example: FAMU's 2026–27 form was due April 30 and asked whether the student completed a current FAFSA.

Easy first step: email the financial aid office: "How does my student apply for the Mary McLeod Bethune Scholarship, and what's the deadline?"
Source: famu.edu Office of Financial Aid (2026–27 form)
