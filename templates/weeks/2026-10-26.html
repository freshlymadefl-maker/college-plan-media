Good to know

3 things parents ask us

It boosts the Bright Futures GPA

Academic dual enrollment gets the same extra weight as AP: an A counts as a 4.5.

The grades are college grades

They start a real college transcript, so start with 1–2 classes.

Each high school sets its own worksheet due date.
