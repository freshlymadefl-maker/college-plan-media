Myth vs. fact

Bright Futures edition

MYTH
If my student qualifies, Bright Futures kicks in automatically.
FACT
Students have to apply.

They must submit the Florida Financial Aid Application (FFAA) by August 31 after graduation. No FFAA, no Bright Futures, even with perfect scores.
