Scholarship of the week

Did you know about…

The Benacquisto Scholarship

WHAT IT CAN COVER
Up to the full cost of attendance
WHO
National Merit Scholars with a standard Florida diploma
WHERE
Eligible Florida colleges, full-time, the fall after graduation
HOW IT STARTS
The PSAT/NMSQT in junior year. That's this month.
TO RENEW
Stay full-time and keep a 3.0 GPA
Source: Florida Statutes s. 1009.893. Award is cost of attendance minus Bright Futures and National Merit awards, and depends on state funding.
