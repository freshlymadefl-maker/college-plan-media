Three Bright Futures mistakes Florida parents make.
Number one: thinking it's automatic. It's not. Your student has to apply through the Florida Financial Aid Application, the F F A A, by August thirty-first after graduation.
Number two: waiting until senior year to log service hours. Hours can count starting the summer after eighth grade.
Number three: using the wrong G P A. Bright Futures uses its own weighted G P A, so ask the school counselor for your student's Bright Futures G P A.
