1. A December test? Decide this week

The regular registration deadline for the Dec 5 SAT is Friday, Nov 20, with late registration until Nov 24. For the Dec 12 ACT, the regular deadline has passed, but late registration runs until Nov 29. A December test isn't required. It makes sense if your junior has been practicing and wants a first real score to plan the spring around. Sources: College Board, ACT.

2. One campus, no pressure

If you're driving somewhere for the holiday and there's a college on the way, stop for 30 minutes. Walk around, grab a snack, notice the size. Is it a city campus or a spread-out one? Does your junior picture themselves there? Campuses are quiet over Thanksgiving, so it's a feel-it-out visit, not an official tour. Not traveling? Check a school's admissions site for a virtual tour; 15 minutes on the couch counts. The goal is just one honest reaction, "I liked that" or "too big," to start a list from.

3. Start the activities list now

The Common App has room for up to 10 activities, with short spaces for each one: 50 characters for the position, 100 for the organization name and 150 for details and accomplishments. Common App also counts family responsibilities, jobs, volunteer work and hobbies, not just school clubs. Source: commonapp.org.

Make a simple note on your junior's phone: the activity, which school years, hours per week, any role or award. Ten minutes now saves a stressful evening in senior fall. If service hours are part of the list, check them against Bright Futures, too. Scholarship to know about for next year

The Mary McLeod Bethune Scholarship is a Florida program worth $3,000 a year at Florida A&M, Bethune-Cookman, Edward Waters and Florida Memorial universities. It requires a 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale in high school subjects that count toward a diploma, and gives priority to students with financial need. Each school runs its own application. If one of these schools interests your junior, every high school year's grades build that GPA, junior year included. Source: Florida Statutes s. 1009.73.

Term of the week: superscore

To superscore is to take a student's best section scores from different test dates and combine them. Example: USF says it "considers your highest submitted section scores across all SAT, ACT or CLT test dates." That means a strong math score in December and a strong reading score in March can work together at schools that superscore. Policies differ by college, so check each one. Source: usf.edu.
