Three quick things for juniors this week: a test registration deadline, a dual enrollment window for two Tampa Bay counties, and USF's newest admission numbers.

This week at a glance

  • Nov 17: Pasco juniors register for spring dual enrollment at PHSC

  • Nov 20: register for the Dec 5 SAT (late registration until Nov 24)

  • Nov 20: Pinellas County Schools students register for spring dual enrollment at SPC

  • Dec 12: next ACT (late registration until Nov 29)

1. The Dec 5 SAT: register by Nov 20

The regular registration deadline for the Dec 5 SAT is Nov 20, with late registration until Nov 24. If your junior wants a winter score to plan around, this is the date. Prefer the ACT? The next one is Dec 12, with late registration until Nov 29. Bright Futures also accepts the CLT. Sources: College Board, ACT.

2. Spring dual enrollment: Pinellas + Pasco

Pinellas: St. Petersburg College's spring 2027 dual enrollment registration deadline for Pinellas County Schools students is Nov 20 (private and home school: Dec 15). There is no cost for the application, tuition or required textbooks, academic classes need a 3.0 unweighted GPA, and the school counselor submits the permission form. Source: spcollege.edu.

Pasco: at Pasco-Hernando State College, juniors register on Nov 17, and public school applications are due Dec 1. Source: accelerated.phsc.edu.

3. USF's newest numbers

USF's admitted freshmen for Fall 2026 had a mid-range GPA of 4.13–4.58, SAT 1290–1420, ACT 28–32 and CLT 92–100. USF uses STARS for self-reported grades and doesn't require an essay. For comparison, UCF's Fall 2026 mid-range was a 4.11–4.57 GPA and a 1310–1420 SAT. These ranges are not cutoffs. Sources: usf.edu, ucf.edu.

Scholarship to bookmark for senior year

The Ron Brown Scholar Program awards $40,000 ($10,000 a year) to Black/African American seniors with strong academics, leadership, service and financial need. This year's application is due Dec 1, so next year's will come early in senior fall. Worth a look now so your junior can plan service and recommendations. Source: ronbrown.org.

Term of the week: mid-range

When a university lists a "mid-range" (or "middle 50%") GPA or test score, it shows where the middle of its admitted class landed. Some admitted students were above it and some below. It's a guide, not a cutoff.
