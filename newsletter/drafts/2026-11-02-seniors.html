1. Submitted? Here's what comes next

Hitting submit on the application isn't always the last step:

  • UF: Early Action applicants complete STARS (UF's self-reported grades) by Sunday, Nov 8. After the deadline, STARS locks, so check it for typos before then.

  • Materials dates: FAMU Early Action materials are due Nov 14, UCF, FGCU and New College on Nov 15, and USF on Nov 16.

  • Every portal: have your senior log in to each school's portal this week and look for a missing-items list (transcripts, test scores, self-reported grades).

Every date is in our free Florida deadline calendar.

2. SAT on Saturday? Pack the bag Friday night

College Board's list for weekend test day:

  • A fully charged testing device with the Bluebook app installed and exam setup done

  • The admission ticket from Bluebook (a printed copy is preferred)

  • An acceptable photo ID: the physical card, not a picture of it

  • Pencils or pens for scratch work, and an approved calculator (Bluebook also has one built in)

Then breakfast, and no post-test interrogation in the car. Source: satsuite.collegeboard.org.

3. Scholarship of the week: José Martí Scholarship Challenge Grant

A Florida state award for Hispanic students: $2,000 a year, renewable with a 3.0 GPA in college. Who qualifies: students of Spanish culture with origins in Mexico, Central or South America, or the Caribbean; U.S. citizens who have been Florida residents for 12 months before fall classes; a 3.0 unweighted high school GPA; enrolling full-time.

How to apply: on the FFAA (the same Florida form as Bright Futures) by April 1 of senior year, with the FAFSA processed error-free by May 15. Priority goes to students with the lowest family resources and funding is limited, so file early. Sources: Florida Statutes s. 1009.72 and Florida Administrative Code Rule 6A-20.023.

Term of the week: Materials deadline

Some Florida schools have two dates for the same admission round: the application deadline (submit the application) and a later materials deadline (transcripts, test scores and self-reported grades must arrive). Missing the second one can hold up a decision.
