1. UCF Early Action isn't done until Nov 15

If your senior applied Early Action to UCF, the application was only step one. By Sunday, Nov 15, UCF needs the SPARK form (Self-Provided Academic Record for Knights, UCF's version of self-reported grades) and official SAT, ACT or CLT scores. UCF says Early Action applicants who meet every deadline get priority for merit scholarships, and decisions come Dec 4. Five minutes in the portal tonight beats a Sunday-night scramble.

2. Still applying? The next deadlines

UNF priority: application and materials by Nov 18 (decisions Dec 9). FSU Regular Decision: apply by Dec 1, materials by Dec 8. USF and FIU: Dec 16. Thanksgiving falls right in the middle, so it helps to plan the writing for the weekend before.

3. When decisions come out

UCF, FAMU and FAU on Dec 4. USF and UNF on Dec 9. FIU on Dec 10. UF Early Decision on Dec 11. FSU Early Decision and Early Action on Dec 17. Every date is in our free Florida college deadline calendar, and it syncs to your phone so nobody has to refresh a portal for four weeks.

Scholarship of the week: Ron Brown Scholar Program

$40,000 ($10,000 a year for four years) for Black/African American high school seniors who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents, with strong academics, leadership, community service and financial need. About 20–25 students are chosen each year. The application is due Dec 1; transcripts and recommendations by Dec 15. Source: ronbrown.org.

Bonus for any senior: Burger King Scholars awards $1,000 to $60,000 to Class of 2027 students with a 2.0 GPA or higher. It closes Dec 15 (11:59pm Central), and only the first 30,000 applications are reviewed. Source: Scholarship America.

Term of the week: self-reported academic record

Many Florida universities ask students to type in their own courses and grades instead of sending a transcript first. UF, FSU and USF call it STARS; UCF calls it SPARK. Enter grades exactly as they appear on the transcript. An official transcript comes after graduation.
