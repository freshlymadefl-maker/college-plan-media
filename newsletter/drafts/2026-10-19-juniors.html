1. Bright Futures takes 3 tests, not 2

Bright Futures Academic and Medallion Scholars accept the SAT, ACT or CLT, and Florida's public universities accept all three for admission too. For the Class of 2028, the minimums went up this year:

  • Florida Academic Scholars: 1350 SAT (was 1330), 29 ACT, or 96 CLT

  • Florida Medallion Scholars: 1200 SAT (was 1190), 25 ACT (was 24), or 83 CLT

GPA and service hour rules didn't change, and your student only needs to hit the minimum on one of the three tests. Seniors have until Aug 31 of their graduation year to test, but junior year is the time to get a qualifying score on the board. Not sure where your student stands? Our tip: pick one test, take a free official practice test at home, and register for a real date before winter break so your student has room for a retake in spring.

3. Spring dual enrollment: ask now

Florida dual enrollment lets high school students take college classes for free: students don't pay registration, tuition or lab fees, and the books are free too. College credit classes need a 3.0 unweighted GPA plus scores that show college readiness. Spring paperwork deadlines are coming up (Hillsborough College's is Nov 6), so ask your school counselor this week.

Term of the week: CLT

The CLT (Classic Learning Test) is a college entrance exam from Classic Learning Initiatives. Florida's Board of Governors lists it alongside the SAT and ACT for university admission, and it counts for Bright Futures Academic and Medallion Scholars (but not Gold Seal Vocational). It also offers remote test dates.

Scholarship of the week, for next fall: the Elks Most Valuable Student scholarship gives 500 four-year awards to U.S. citizen seniors, and this year's deadline is Nov 12. It's a good one to put on your calendar for senior year (elks.org).
