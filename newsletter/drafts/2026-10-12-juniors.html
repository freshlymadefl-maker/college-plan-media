1. Pick a winter test date now

The Class of 2028 needs a higher score for Bright Futures than this year's seniors: SAT 1350, ACT 29 or CLT 96 for Florida Academic Scholars, and SAT 1200, ACT 25 or CLT 83 for Florida Medallion Scholars. Your student only needs one qualifying score.

  • ACT Dec 12: register by Nov 6 (late registration until Nov 29)

  • SAT Dec 5: register by Nov 20 (late until Nov 24). There's no SAT in January or February; the next one is March 6.

  • ACT Feb 27: register by Jan 22

A winter test leaves room for a spring retake, and summer, before senior year.

2. Watch the seniors: this is your preview

This time next year, your student will face the same early deadlines. Florida's big ones for this year's seniors: Oct 15 (UF Early Decision, FSU Early Decision/Early Action, UCF and FAU Early Action) and Nov 1–2 (UF Early Action, FIU, USF and others). Dates can shift a little each year, but the rhythm doesn't. A rough college list by spring makes next fall much calmer.

See every date in our free Florida deadline calendar.

3. Thinking about dual enrollment for spring?

Spring paperwork deadlines come up fast. In Hillsborough County, for example, Hillsborough College's spring dual enrollment paperwork is due Nov 6, and late paperwork isn't accepted. If your student is interested, ask the school counselor this week about your district's partner college and its deadline.

Scholarship of the week: Benacquisto

The Benacquisto Scholarship is a Florida program for National Merit Scholars. For Florida residents at an eligible Florida school, it can cover up to the full cost of attendance (after Bright Futures and National Merit awards, and depending on state funding). The path starts with the PSAT/NMSQT, which many juniors are taking this month. Source: Florida Statutes s. 1009.893.

Term of the week: Superscore

A superscore combines your student's best section scores from different test dates into one new total. UF, for example, superscores test scores. Each college sets its own policy, so check before deciding how many times to test.

Dates and policies can change, so always confirm on each school's official admissions page and on FloridaBrightFutures.gov. Questions? Just hit reply. We read every email.
