1. Bright Futures test scores are going up for your student's class

The Florida Department of Education raised the SAT scores needed for Bright Futures, starting with the Class of 2028:

  • Florida Academic Scholars (100% tuition): SAT 1350, up from 1330

  • Florida Medallion Scholars (75% tuition): SAT 1200, up from 1190

Some ACT and CLT minimums changed too. Ask your school counselor for the official chart for your student's class.

What didn't change: a 3.5 weighted GPA plus 100 service hours for Academic Scholars, and a 3.0 weighted GPA plus 75 service hours for Medallion. Both also allow 100 paid work hours or a combination of work and service.

2. It's PSAT month

Many Florida high schools give the PSAT/NMSQT sometime between Oct 1 and Oct 30. For juniors, this is the test that counts for National Merit recognition. If your student has already taken it, the score report will show which skills to work on before the real SAT.

3. Pick a first SAT date

Having a plan beats cramming. Upcoming SAT dates:

  • Dec 5: register by Nov 20

  • March 6, 2027: register by Feb 19

Many students take their first official SAT in the winter or spring of junior year, then retake it if needed.

Term of the week: Weighted GPA

A weighted GPA gives extra points for harder classes like Honors, AP, IB, AICE and dual enrollment. Bright Futures uses its own weighted GPA calculation, which can differ from the GPA on your student's report card. Ask the counselor for your student's "Bright Futures GPA."
