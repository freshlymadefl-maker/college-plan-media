Junior year is the last full year of grades colleges see before senior applications. This week: what Florida's two most popular universities actually admitted, the winter test dates, and a spring dual enrollment heads-up.

This week at a glance

  • Know: UF and FSU's newest admitted-student numbers

  • Do: pick a winter test date (first registration deadline: Nov 5)

  • Ask: your counselor about spring dual enrollment

1. What UF and FSU admitted this year

Both schools publish the middle 50% of the students they admitted (half were inside the range, a quarter above, a quarter below):

  • UF, Class of 2030: GPA 4.5–4.7, SAT 1420–1520, ACT 32–35. UF says it received 95,986 applications and admitted 19,012.

  • FSU, Fall 2026: core GPA 4.3–4.6, SAT 1380–1480, ACT 31–34.

Both review applications holistically, so essays, activities and course rigor matter too. But the GPA ranges show why this year's grades carry so much weight. Sources: admissions.ufl.edu and admissions.fsu.edu.

2. Pick a winter test date

  • CLT (remote option): Nov 14, register by Nov 5. Dec 8, register by Dec 3.

  • SAT: Dec 5, register by Nov 20.

  • ACT: Dec 12, register by Nov 6.

Bright Futures and Florida's public universities accept all three. For the Class of 2028, Academic Scholars needs a 1350 SAT, 29 ACT or 96 CLT, and Medallion needs 1200, 25 or 83. Not sure where your student stands? Try our Bright Futures checker.

3. Spring dual enrollment deadlines are close

Spring sign-ups run now through early December at many Florida colleges. Two examples: Hillsborough College's spring paperwork is due Nov 6, and Pasco-Hernando State College's spring application is due Dec 1 (registration Nov 17 for juniors). College credit classes need a 3.0 unweighted GPA plus college-ready test scores, and tuition and books are free. Ask your counselor which college partners with your district.

Scholarship to bookmark for next fall

The Equitable Excellence Scholarship ($5,000 a year, renewable) is open to seniors at public high schools with a 2.5 GPA or higher. This year's deadline is Dec 22, so expect a similar window in your student's senior fall.

Term of the week: Middle 50%

The middle 50% is the range between the 25th and 75th percentile of admitted (or enrolled) students. A score inside the range is typical for that school; a score below it doesn't rule your student out, especially with strong grades and rigor.
