1. The short list before Thanksgiving

If your senior applied Early Action to USF, the application was step one. USF needs the materials, including the STARS self-reported academic record and test scores, by Monday, Nov 16. USF says an application is incomplete until STARS and scores arrive.

UNF priority applicants need the application and materials in by Wednesday, Nov 18. Still want one more SAT score? Registration for the Dec 5 SAT closes Nov 20 (late registration until Nov 24). One catch: USF's site says fall applicants should test before December to get scores in on time, so check each school's score deadline before paying for a December test.

Applying to FSU Regular Decision? The application is due Dec 1, the Tuesday after Thanksgiving, with materials due Dec 8. If the writing isn't done, plan it for this weekend, not the holiday.

2. When decisions come out

  • Dec 4: UCF, FAU and FAMU (Early Action)

  • Dec 9: USF (Early Action) and UNF (priority)

  • Dec 10: FIU (Early Action)

  • Dec 11: UF (Early Decision)

  • Dec 17: FSU (Early Decision and Early Action)

Every date is in our free Florida college deadline calendar, and it can sync to your phone. Still deciding where to apply in December? USF Regular Decision, FIU and UNF's standard deadline all fall on Dec 16. Our guide to all 12 Florida public university deadlines has the rest.

3. "So, where is she applying?"

Someone will ask at Thanksgiving dinner. For a senior who is waiting on decisions, it can feel like a test. A few ideas that help:

  • Agree on a line before you go. Something like "She applied to a few Florida schools and hears back in December. We're excited!" Your senior decides how much to share, not you.

  • Skip the scores and the ranking. GPA, SAT and "first choice" don't need to come up at the table.

  • Redirect to something they love. "Ask him about the robotics team" turns an interview into a conversation.

  • Give them an exit. A senior who wants to go throw a football with the cousins is doing just fine.

And remember: decisions in December are a beginning, not a verdict. Many students land happily somewhere that wasn't on their original list.

Scholarship of the week: Mary McLeod Bethune Scholarship

A Florida program worth $3,000 a year for students at four schools: Florida A&M University, Bethune-Cookman University, Edward Waters University and Florida Memorial University. The state law behind it (section 1009.73, Florida Statutes) requires a 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale in high school subjects that count toward a diploma, acceptance at one of the four schools and full-time enrollment. Priority goes to students with financial need, and the state rule adds Florida residency. To renew, students keep a 3.0 cumulative GPA and complete 12 credits each term they receive it.

Each school runs its own application and deadline. For example, FAMU's 2026–27 form was due April 30 and asks for a current FAFSA. If one of these schools is on your senior's list, ask its financial aid office for the 2027–28 form. Sources: Florida Statutes s. 1009.73; Florida Administrative Code 6A-20.029; FAMU Office of Financial Aid.

Two more with December deadlines: the Equitable Excellence Scholarship ($5,000 a year, renewable; public high school seniors with a 2.5+ GPA) closes Dec 22 at 3pm Central, and Burger King Scholars ($1,000 to $60,000; Class of 2027, 2.0+ GPA) closes Dec 15 at 11:59pm Central. Source: Scholarship America.

Term of the week: materials deadline

Many Florida universities have two dates for each round: an application deadline and a later materials deadline for things like the self-reported grades (STARS or SPARK) and official test scores. Submitting the application doesn't make it complete. The applicant portal shows what is still missing, so it's worth a look after every deadline.
