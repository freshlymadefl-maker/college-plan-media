3. Scholarship of the week: Elks Most Valuable Student

The Elks Most Valuable Student scholarship gives 500 four-year awards to high school seniors. According to the Elks National Foundation's current awards table, the top 20 receive $30,000 and the other 480 receive $4,000 (four-year totals).

  • Open to current high school seniors who are U.S. citizens

  • No Elks connection needed: you don't have to be related to a member

  • Deadline: Nov 12, 11:59pm Pacific (that's 2:59am Nov 13 in Florida, but aim for the weekend before)

Details at elks.org. Also open now: Burger King Scholars ($1,000 to $60,000 for seniors), open until Dec 15 at burgerkingfoundation.org.

Term of the week: Early Action vs. Early Decision

Early Action means applying early and hearing back early, with no promise to attend. Early Decision is binding: if admitted, your student commits to that school (FSU, for example, lists its Early Decision as binding, with a Jan 15 deposit). Read each school's rules before your senior clicks submit.
