1. The only deadlines before Dec 1

Testing. If your senior still wants one more score, late registration for the Dec 5 SAT closes Tuesday, Nov 24 at 11:59pm ET (College Board says extra fees may apply). Late registration for the Dec 12 ACT closes Sunday, Nov 29.

FSU. The Regular Decision application is due Tuesday, Dec 1. If your student wants the FSU Honors Program, that's a separate step: answer "Yes" to the Honors question on the FSU application, then submit the Honors supplemental application (a 250 to 350 word response). It also closes Dec 1, and Honors decisions come Feb 18, 2027.

3. Scholarship of the week: the Florida Student Assistance Grant

The FSAG is Florida's need-based grant for students at Florida public universities and state colleges, and there's no separate application: it starts with the FAFSA. UF, USF and FIU all list the FAFSA as required, and USF says to file by its published priority deadline because each school awards from a set amount of state money.

  • Who: Florida residents (UF lists 12 months before the school year starts), U.S. citizens or eligible noncitizens, with financial need (USF says Pell Grant eligible), degree-seeking and enrolled in at least 6 credit hours.

  • How much: UF lists $200 to $2,610 a year; FIU lists up to $2,200 a year for full-time students. The award letter shows the real number.

  • Don't mix it up: FSAG is need-based and comes from the FAFSA. Bright Futures is merit-based and comes from the FFAA. Most Florida seniors should file both.

Sources: s. 1009.50, Florida Statutes; sfa.ufl.edu; usf.edu/financial-aid; onestop.fiu.edu.

Term of the week: Early Action vs. Early Decision

Early Action means applying early and hearing early, with no promise to attend. Early Decision is binding: if admitted, the student agrees to attend and withdraws other applications. UF's December round is Early Decision; most other Florida public universities use Early Action. Check each school's own terms.
