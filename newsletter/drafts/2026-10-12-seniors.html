1. Submitting Oct 15? The job isn't done yet

For many Florida schools, the application is step one. The materials come a week later:

  • UF: STARS (UF's self-reported grades record), transcript and test scores are due Oct 22 for Early Decision. Every freshman applicant fills out STARS, and the Common App's courses-and-grades section does not replace it. UF also requires an SAT, ACT or CLT score (self-reporting is fine).

  • FSU: Early Decision/Early Action materials are due Oct 22; test scores by Dec 1.

  • FAU: Early Action materials are due Oct 22.

  • UCF: Early Action materials are due Nov 15.

Do this: The weekend after submitting, have your senior log in to each school's applicant portal and check for anything marked missing.

2. The Nov 1–2 wave

If your senior is applying early to any of these, the deadline is about three weeks out:

  • Nov 1: UF Early Action (non-binding; STARS + materials Nov 8), FAMU, FGCU and New College Early Action, University of Miami Early Decision I and Early Action, Stetson Early Decision

  • Nov 2: FIU Early Action (application and documents) and USF Early Action

Every one of these dates is in our free Florida deadline calendar, which you can add to your phone in one tap.

3. Scholarship of the week: Jack Kent Cooke

The Jack Kent Cooke College Scholarship awards up to $55,000 a year for college. It's "last-dollar" funding, added after a college's own aid. Seniors can apply if they:

  • Graduate in spring 2027 and start at a four-year college in fall 2027

  • Have a cumulative unweighted GPA of 3.75 or higher

  • Have a family income up to $95,000

  • Have at least one SAT, ACT, AP or IB score (no minimum)

  • Attended all four years of high school in the U.S.

It's on the Common App and needs two teacher recommendations (one from an 11th grade core teacher) and a counselor recommendation. Deadline: Nov 11. Ask for those letters this week. Details at jkcf.org.

One more with a November deadline: the Elks Most Valuable Student scholarship (U.S. citizen seniors) is due Nov 12 at 11:59pm Pacific. See elks.org.

Term of the week: STARS

STARS is UF's Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System. Your student types in their courses and grades from their transcript and links it to their UF application in the Gator Portal. It's required, and it has its own deadline: one week after the application deadline.
