1. What UCF's freshmen looked like

UCF publishes the middle 50% of its fall entering freshman class:

  • Core GPA: 4.11–4.57 (UCF's own recalculation)

  • SAT: 1310–1420 · ACT: 28–32 · CLT: 91–101

UCF superscores the SAT and ACT, and admitted freshmen are automatically considered for UCF scholarships. Source: ucf.edu/admissions.

2. Why your junior has 3 different GPAs

  • The report card GPA uses your district's scale.

  • The Bright Futures GPA counts 16 core credits. Honors, AP, IB, AICE and dual enrollment courses each add 0.5.

  • University GPAs are recalculated too. UF and UCF add a full point for AP, IB, AICE and dual enrollment, and half a point for honors (and UCF also for pre-AP, pre-IB and pre-AICE).

So the same transcript can show three numbers. Check where your student stands for Bright Futures with our free Bright Futures checker. Sources: Bright Futures Student Handbook, admissions.ufl.edu, ucf.edu/admissions.

3. Pick a winter test date

  • ACT: Dec 12, register by Fri Nov 6 (late registration through Nov 29)

  • SAT: Dec 5, register by Nov 20

  • CLT (remote option): Dec 8, register by Dec 3

Bright Futures and Florida's public universities accept all three.

Scholarship to bookmark for next fall

The José Martí Scholarship Challenge Grant gives Hispanic Florida students with a 3.0 unweighted GPA $2,000 a year. It's applied for on the FFAA by April 1 of senior year, so put it on next year's list. Source: Florida Statutes s. 1009.72.

Term of the week: Superscore

When a college superscores, it takes your student's best section scores from different test dates and combines them. UCF superscores both the SAT and the ACT, which is one reason a second winter test can be worth it.
