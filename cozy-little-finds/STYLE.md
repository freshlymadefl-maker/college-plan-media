# Cozy Little Finds (@cozylittlefindsdaily) style notes

## Holiday look: "A Very Cozy Christmas" (owner, Nov 23 2026)
Inspired by a vintage "campy Christmas" carousel the owner loved: "perfect combo of classy and funny and vintage."
- Cover: tinsel curtain, a big soft retro serif title (Fraunces Black, SOFT/WONK on), gold italic second line, a short deadpan sub-line.
- Voice: deadpan, a little dramatic. The joke goes in the small italic line under each find ("subtle is for January").
- Texture: film grain and warm faded edges on every slide.
- Palette: Santa red #B3261E, velvet #6E1414, tinsel gold #D4A84B, pine #1F4D3A, retro teal #2F7F86, eggnog #F6EBD9.
- Patterns: red/green plaid, velvet, gold double frames, glass ornaments.
- Photos: only her own styled flat-lays or properly licensed vintage photos. Never repost other accounts' images.
- Template: templates/campy-xmas.html (?s=cover|list|joke|sheet), render with templates/render.py. Fonts are in fonts/ (OFL).
- Samples: 2026-11-campy-xmas/ (the list items are placeholder ideas, not real picks yet).
